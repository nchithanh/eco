# Product

**Dolphin Ops** is an **Agent CRM** (SaaS) for owners and staff. Internal operations — not the customer-facing website chatbot (that is Dolphin Care, marketing-only in this repo).

## Model

User intent → AI Agent → Business Tool → appropriate UI → user interaction → action.

Chat is **one** entry layer, not the whole product.

## Contrast

- Not a menu CRM with chat bolted on
- Not a ChatGPT wrapper
- Not chat-only (ask → text → ask again)

Signature: **AI does not replace the UI. It chooses the right UI.**

## Care vs Ops

| | Care (marketing site) | Ops (this app) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Face | Customer | Owner / staff |
| Where | Business website | Internal operations |

Do not introduce Dolphin Intelligence in Ops product UI or copy.

## Wedge

Service businesses, order: **Spa → Salon → Clinic → Shop dịch vụ**. Do not size the product as “small team only”.
